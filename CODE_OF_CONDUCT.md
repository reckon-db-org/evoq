# Code of Conduct

evoq is maintained in the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1, which we adopt here.

In short: be decent to the people you are working with. Harassment, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, and sustained disruption are not welcome, here
or in any space where people are working on evoq. Technical disagreement is
welcome; it is the point.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, commit messages and reviews in this
repository, and to any other space where someone is participating in evoq on
behalf of this project.

## Reporting

Write to the maintainer at <raf.lefever@erlef.org>. Reports are handled
privately, and the reporter's identity is not shared without their consent.

The maintainer may warn, ask for a correction, remove a contribution, or block
an account, according to what the situation needs. There is no formal committee
and no appeal body: this is a small project, and the maintainer's decision is
the decision.
